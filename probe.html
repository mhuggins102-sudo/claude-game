How to play

A 5-letter word is hidden. Spend your 7 tokens on clues:

• Test a letter (1) — tap a chip to learn if it's in the word.
• Reveal a slot (2) — uncover the exact letter in a position.
• Count vowels (1) — how many vowels it has.

When ready, type your answer and SOLVE. A wrong solve costs a token — run out and the case goes cold. Fewer tokens spent = higher score.
