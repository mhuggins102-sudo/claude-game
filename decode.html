How to play

Every letter in the quote has been swapped for another — the same way every time. Your job is to undo it.

• Tap a letter, then type your guess for what it really is. Every matching letter fills in.
• Use letter frequency & short words (THE, A, IS) as footholds.
• Hint reveals one letter if you're stuck.
