How to play

Build words of 4+ letters using only the seven letters in the hive.

• Every word must include the center letter (gold).
• Letters can be reused.
• A word's value comes from its length × its rarity — long, uncommon words are worth the most.
• A pangram (all 7 letters) earns a big bonus.
• No plurals — there's never an S. Other forms (-ed, -ing, -er) count when they're real words.

Reach the goal score to clear the round, then keep climbing for a higher rank. Words are everyday vocabulary — no obscure stumpers. No timer.
