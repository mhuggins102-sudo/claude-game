How to play

Build words of 4+ letters using only the seven letters in the hive.

• Every word must include the center letter (gold).
• Letters can be reused.
• A word's value = its length × its rarity. Everyday words count toward the goal; rarer words are still accepted for bonus points.
• A pangram (all 7 letters) earns a big bonus.
• No plurals — there's never an S. Other forms (-ed, -ing, -er) count when they're real words.

Relaxed: reach the goal score to clear, then keep climbing for a higher rank — no timer.
Marathon: a running clock. Hit each round's goal to bank more time; the target rises every round until the clock beats you.
