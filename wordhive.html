How to play

Build words of 4+ letters using only the seven letters in the hive.

• Every word must include the center letter (gold).
• Letters can be reused.
• Longer words score more; a pangram uses all 7 letters for a big bonus.

Climb the ranks from Beginner to Genius. There's no timer — take your time.
